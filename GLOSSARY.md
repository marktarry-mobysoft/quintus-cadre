# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A user who buys and takes courses. A Learner may raise a Refund Request.

**Admin**:
A platform administrator. The only person who may approve or decline a Refund Request, because they have no stake in the course's quality. An Admin never decides a Refund Request they raised themselves.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, the team, staff, Instructor (for refund decisions)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days from purchase in which a learner may raise a Refund Request. A Declined Refund Request doesn't reset or extend it.

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it ends the learner's access to the course. A learner has at most one Approved Refund Request per course, ever, even if they buy the course again.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner is told. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund for an Approved Refund Request where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Overdue Refund**:
A Pending Refund that still isn't Issued five working days after approval, which is the time the learner was promised.

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
